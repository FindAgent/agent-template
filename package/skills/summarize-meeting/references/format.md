# Output format

- Headings are level 2 and spelled exactly `Decisions`, `Action items` and `Open questions`.
- A section with nothing to report keeps its heading and says `None stated.`
- An action item is `owner: task (due date)`. A missing owner is `unassigned`; a missing date is left out and not guessed.
- Dates stay as the notes wrote them. Do not convert "next Friday" into a calendar date unless the notes give the meeting date.
